A Masquerade Prom with a Touch of Vintage Carnival Magic

Step into The Midnight Carnival of Masques, a formal masquerade prom with just enough vintage carnival magic to make the night unforgettable.

Homeschool students, their friends, and guests from near or far are welcome to join us for dinner, desserts, a DJ, dancing, a photo booth, and an unforgettable prom night.

Dress formal or semi-formal and embrace the masquerade theme as much or as little as you'd like. Masks are encouraged, not required.

South Texas Homeschool Prom & Formal

South Texas Homeschool Prom & Formal is a nonprofit organization created to give homeschool students opportunities to experience the celebrations, friendships and milestones that make these years unforgettable.

Homeschooling may look different from a traditional school experience, but that shouldn't mean missing the moments students look forward to.

Our events welcome homeschool students from communities near and far, giving them a place to come together, meet new people, celebrate and make memories.

Our mission is simple: to bring homeschool students together and give them a place to belong, celebrate, make new friends, and create the kind of memories they'll talk about for years to come.

Why We Do It

We believe every student deserves the chance to dress up, dance, laugh and experience a truly special night, no matter where they call home.
