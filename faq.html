Before the Masks Go On...

Everything you need to know about attending The Midnight Carnival of Masques. Click any question below to reveal the answer.

Attendance & Guests

Who can attend?

The Midnight Carnival of Masques is open to homeschool students who are at least 14 years old and currently in 9th through 12th grade. Students are welcome from South Texas and beyond.

Can I bring a guest who isn't homeschooled?

Yes. An eligible homeschool student may bring a non-homeschooled guest. Guests follow the same waiver, dress code, conduct, and event rules.

Can my guest already be graduated?

Yes. Recent graduates may attend as the guest of an eligible student if they graduated within the previous two years and are no older than 19 on May 1, 2027.

For the 2027 prom, graduates from 2025 or 2026 may attend as guests if they meet the age requirement.

Do I need a date?

Nope! Come with a date, a friend, a group, or on your own. A Pair ticket is simply discounted admission for two people. You do not have to be a couple.

Tickets & Registration

How much are tickets?

When is the registration deadline?

Please register by April 1 whenever possible. Registrations after April 1 may have an additional late-registration fee and limited meal options as we finalize catering, seating, security, memorabilia, and other arrangements.

Registration closes completely April 21, 2027. No registrations will be accepted after this date.

Are tickets refundable or transferable?

Tickets are non-refundable, but they may be transferred to another eligible student. Contact us so we can update the attendee information and required paperwork.

The Attire: Midnight Elegance Behind the Mask

What is the dress code?

This is prom, and we want it to feel like prom.

The dress code is Formal Prom Attire, with polished Semi-Formal Attire accepted. Black tie is optional.

The Midnight Carnival aesthetic is dark, rich, and old-world. Think jewel tones, burgundy, midnight blue, black, antique gold, velvet, satin, lace, brocade, and dramatic vintage details.

The theme is optional. Individuality is encouraged.

What should ladies wear?

Floor-length evening gowns and formal prom gowns are encouraged. Full-length formal alternatives are also welcome.

Attire must provide appropriate coverage while standing, sitting, bending, and dancing. Slits may not extend to the hip bone, and appropriate coverage must be maintained throughout the evening.

What should gentlemen wear?

Tuxedos are welcome but not required.

Suits, dress slacks, dress shirts, ties or bow ties, sport coats, velvet jackets, waistcoats, and other polished formal or semi-formal attire are welcome.

Undergarments should not be visible, pants should be worn properly, and casual shirts such as T-shirts, tank tops, or muscle shirts do not meet the dress code.

Is Texas Formal okay?

Yes, but we're talking formal Texas Formal.

Dress shirts, formal jackets, polished western boots, and western hats are welcome.

If you're wearing denim, it must be BLACK denim only. No blue denim, regardless of how dark it is.

And those jeans better be clean, sharply pressed, and starched enough to practically stand on their own, with creases sharp enough to slice a steak.

Everyday jeans, faded or distressed denim, and casual jeans-and-boots outfits do not meet the dress code.

Does formal attire have to be expensive?

Absolutely not. Thrift it. Borrow it. Swap it. Alter it. Restyle something you already own.

Check resale and consignment shops, community formalwear programs, giveaways, and online resale groups. Beautiful prom attire can be found for very little or even completely free if you know where to look.

We want the dress code to make the night feel special, not make attending financially out of reach.

A Little Note From Us

Make the evening your own.

This is your prom, so have fun with it. Dress outside the norm! Be elegant, dramatic, vintage, romantic, gothic, glamorous, culturally expressive, unconventional, or classic.

Whether your style leans classic Texas, old-world masquerade, modern formal, or somewhere completely unexpected, make it yours.

Just follow the dress and coverage guidelines above, and come ready for prom.

Do I have to wear a mask or dress to the theme?

No. Masquerade masks and Midnight Carnival styling are encouraged, not required.

This is a dark, vintage carnival masquerade, not a circus or costume party. Think candlelight, velvet, jewel tones, antique gold, mysterious masks, and old-world carnival elegance.

Carnival flair is welcome. Circus costumes are not.

The Evening

What's included with my ticket?

Your ticket includes a full-course dinner, dessert bar, drinks, DJ and dancing, photo booth, and prom activities and entertainment.

What time is prom? Can parents come in for photos?

Doors open at 5:15 PM for check-in and photos. Prom is 6:00–10:00 PM.

Parents are welcome inside during check-in and photos but must leave by 6:00 PM unless they are registered volunteers or chaperones.

Can I request a song?

Yes! Submit song requests on the event waiver or email stx.homeschoolprom@gmail.com. Requests remain subject to event and DJ approval.

Dinner & Dietary Needs

What about allergies or dietary restrictions?

We'll collect food allergy and dietary information during registration and on the required waiver.

Dinner will be gluten-free or include a gluten-free option, and we make every effort for food and refreshments to be free from artificial dyes.

We cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment, so please tell us about any dietary needs.

Waivers, Check-In & Leaving

Is a waiver required?

Yes. Every attending student must have a completed event waiver to enter.

Students will also sign in when they arrive, including whether they drove themselves or arrived with someone else.

Can students leave during prom?

Students may not leave the building or go outside without chaperone approval and supervision.

A student leaving early will be considered checked out and is no longer under event supervision. South Texas Homeschool Prom & Formal is not responsible for their transportation or whom they leave with after checkout.

If an unexpected early departure occurs, a parent/guardian or emergency contact may be contacted.

Can students come back after leaving?

Re-entry is not automatic. A student who has left or checked out may re-enter only with approval from a chaperone or event organizer.

Safety & Conduct

How is the event supervised?

The event is supervised by adult chaperones. Volunteers and chaperones working with or supervising students are background checked.

What behavior is expected?

Respect yourself, fellow students, chaperones, volunteers, and event organizers.

Vulgar, threatening, discriminatory, sexually inappropriate, disruptive, or otherwise inappropriate behavior is not permitted. Students must follow event rules and reasonable instructions from chaperones and organizers.

What items are prohibited?

Weapons, alcohol, illegal drugs, tobacco products, vaping products, related paraphernalia, and items that create a safety concern or substantially disrupt the event are prohibited.

What is the three-strike policy?

Ordinary behavioral or rule violations will generally follow a three-strike policy.

Serious violations, including weapons or prohibited items, violence, threats, harassment, dangerous behavior, or other serious misconduct, may result in immediate removal without three warnings.

Students removed from the event may not return that evening.

Prom Court

Who can participate in Prom Court?

Juniors and seniors attending the 2027 prom may enter themselves or be nominated by a friend.

Candidates will submit a photo, short bio, a little about themselves, and why they would like to be your 2027 Prom King or Prom Queen.

Full details and candidates will be available on our Prom Court page.

How does voting work?

Voting takes place in person at prom and is open only to students attending the event. There is no online voting.
