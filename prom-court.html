Think the Crown Belongs to You?

Or maybe you know someone who absolutely needs to see themselves in one. Either way, don't be shy!

Juniors and seniors attending the 2027 prom are invited to enter our Prom Court for Prom King or Prom Queen.

✦ ◆ ✦

How to Enter

Ready to throw your name in for the crown? Juniors and seniors attending prom may nominate themselves or be nominated by a friend.

To officially enter Prom Court:

  • Select YES when asked on your event Google Form if you'd like to participate in Prom Court.
  • Email your Prom Court submission to stx.homeschoolprom@gmail.com.

Your email should include:

  • A photo of yourself
  • A short bio so everyone can get to know you
  • A few words about why you'd like to be the 2027 Prom King or Prom Queen

Your reason doesn't have to be serious or impressive. Tell us why you'd love the crown, make us laugh, tell us something memorable about yourself, or simply admit that you really want to see yourself wearing one.

Prom Court submissions must be received by April 21, 2027.

That's the same final cutoff as prom registration, so make sure everything is in by the deadline!

Meet the Court

Prom Court nominations will be featured online before the event, including each candidate's photo and bio, so students can get to know this year's court.

On prom night, we'll also have a Prom Court table displaying all of our nominees, their photos, and their bios before voting begins.

Voting Happens at Prom

No online voting.

Every student attending prom gets one vote.

Voting will take place in person during the event, and when the votes are counted, we'll crown our 2027 Prom King and Prom Queen!

What's Waiting for the Winners?

Besides getting to walk away as the 2027 Prom King or Prom Queen, our winners will each receive:

  • A crown
  • A Prom King or Prom Queen sash
  • Gift cards

And, of course, permanent bragging rights.
