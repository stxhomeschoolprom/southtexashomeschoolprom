How to Enter

Ready to throw your name in for the crown? Juniors and seniors attending prom may nominate themselves or be nominated by a friend.

To officially enter Prom Court:

  • Select YES when asked on your event Google Form if you'd like to participate in Prom Court.
  • Email your Prom Court submission to stx.homeschoolprom@gmail.com.

Your email should include:

  • A photo of yourself
  • A short bio so everyone can get to know you
  • A few words about why you'd like to be the 2027 Prom King or Prom Queen

Your reason doesn't have to be serious or impressive. Tell us why you'd love the crown, make us laugh, tell us something memorable about yourself, or simply admit that you really want to see yourself wearing one.

Prom Court submissions must be received by April 21, 2027. That's the same final cutoff as prom registration, so don't wait until the clock strikes midnight!
